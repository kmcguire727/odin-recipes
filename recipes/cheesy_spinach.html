Cheesy Spinach Recipe

Back to Odin Recipe Page

Description

Cheesy spinach casserole is a warm, baked side dish made by combining spinach with a rich mixture of melted cheeses, eggs, and cottage cheese

Ingredients

  • 4 Eggs
  • 3/4 cup Whole Wheat Flour
  • 3-10oz bags of Frozen, Chopped Spinach
  • 24oz Cottage Cheese
  • 8oz Cheddar Cheese (grated)
  • 3/4 tsp Kohser Salt

Cooking Instructions

  1. Thaw spinach and drain well. Press out excess water.
  2. Beat eggs, salt, and flour together.
  3. Add drained spinach, cottage cheese, 6 oz of cheddar.
  4. Mix lightly to combine ingredients.
  5. Spread into well greased 9x13 casserole dish.
  6. Sprinkle remaining cheddar on top.
  7. Bake uncovered at 350° for 50-60 minutes.
